About Us

BHAIYA GROUP of Industries is one of the leading indigenous and traditional industrial families of the country. This renowned industrial family has been working tirelessly for the welfare of the country and its people with enviable success and reputation since the pre-independence period of the country till now. Bhaiya Group’s contribution to domestic business- trade and industrial development is undeniable. Bhaiya Hotels & Resorts Ltd. is a part of the Bhaiya Group of Industries family. The expansion, success and activity of Bhaiya Housing in the housing sector is also eye-catching.
The Eden Bay Hotel in Kalatali, Cox’s Bazar, one of the best tourist cities of Bangladesh and the world, is a new addition to the Bhaiya Group in the field of tourism accommodation services. At present, there is a shortage of high-quality smart accommodation for tourists in Cox’s Bazar. Therefore, this timely initiative of Bhaiya Hotels & Resorts Ltd. is to provide domestic and foreign tourists with luxurious and beautiful vacations in world-class hotels in Cox’s Bazar. Bhaiya Hotels & Resorts Ltd. strongly believes that their Eden Bay Hotel will become the first choice ‘Tourist Accommodation’ for tourists visiting Cox’s Bazar in the near future. At the same time, Eden Bay will become very commercially important.
